## Track A — Gap 5: common-mode failure detection (most dangerous)

The survey's gap, in one line: triangulation counts agreement as confirmation
without first measuring whether the "independent" perspectives share
instruments, training, data, or incentives. Independence is asserted, not
measured — and instant communication destroys it (Zollman; peer review;
prediction-market frames in the survey).

## Track B — Gap 3: a registry of floors (most buildable)

The survey's gap: nobody maintains a living, stranger-inspectable register
of "distinctions currently treated as fundamental, and who is treating them
so" — with consequences attached when a floor is shown constructed.
Registered Reports register studies; nothing registers frames.

What already exists, ready to be the seed — no new writing needed:
the eight Floor Cards in `floor-cards.md`, each with name, primitives,
native question, sayable/unsayable, tell, canonical probe, cost of
crossing, and expected residue. Examples: Rung-1 Association, Proof (Lean),
Priced Cognition (Marketplace), The Unnameable / This (Madhyamaka),
Bayesian, Evolutionary, Social, Reactive.
