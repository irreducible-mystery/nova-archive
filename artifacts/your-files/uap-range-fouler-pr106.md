## The video (PR106, Eastern United States, 2020, NORTHCOM to AARO)

- Infrared, grayscale, pillarboxed (black bars left/right). A small bright elongated blob sits just above the crosshair and drifts slowly; in the enlarged crop it is a small white lozenge, a few pixels across, with no resolvable shape. By ~4–5 s the reticle changes from a crosshair to a square tracking box in the centre, and the blob is no longer visible in it.
- A small bright square dot persists at the very top centre of the frame throughout. Unidentified — could be a sensor marker; I do not claim it is a second object.
- **No telemetry anywhere in any sampled frame.** No altitude, range, speed, coordinates, time, zoom, or sensor-mode text — only the reticle. This is the cleanest missing-numbers case of the ten videos so far: the earlier nine had Go Fast's 4.1 NM and Aguadilla's numbers; this one has nothing at all to compute with.
- Caveats: stills sampled at 1 fps, not continuous motion, so I cannot verify the dynamics (speed, direction change) directly. There is an AAC audio stream in the file; I did not analyse it.

## The debrief (D089, Range Fouler Debrief Form)

- Reporter: Pilot, rank O-4. Name, squadron, date, time of detection, side number, BuNo, mission description, contact working area, latitude/longitude, contact altitude, wind — all redacted or blank.
- "Was the contact moving?" — **Yes**. Direction/Speed field blank.
- Checkboxes — only two checked: **Metallic ✓** and **Reflective ✓**. Unchecked: Round, Square, Balloon-shaped, Wings/Airframe, Other Shape, Apparent Propulsion, Moving Parts, Markings, Translucent, Opaque.
- Narrative, verbatim apart from redactions: "[redacted] apparent object also captured with [redacted]. Appeared quite small. Speed appeared to be [redacted] and continued in constant direction. Shape indiscernible but the bottom was reflective. [redacted]"
- Page 2 is release authority only (44 U.S.C. 2107; AARO; E.O. 13526 §1.4(a) and (e)).

## Why this one matters to her thread

It is the pattern from the nine-video analysis in its purest form (source:31): a pilot's own words say something small, metallic, reflective-bottomed moved in a constant direction — and the paired video carries no number that could turn that into speed, size, or range. The speed the pilot saw is itself redacted out of the narrative. The absence here is not a sensor-mode limit (as with Gimbal/FLIR1's missing laser range) and not a stripped metadata stream (as with Mosul); the video simply never had telemetry burned in, and the debrief's numbers are black bars.
