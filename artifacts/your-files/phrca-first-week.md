# PHR-CA First Week — start with $0, no exam date needed

Prepared 2026-11-14 (Saturday) for Kelly. **Week 1 runs Monday Nov 23 – Sunday Nov 29, 2026** — the week she can start blocking out her schedule. This is the piece the starter pack
didn't have: a concrete first week that needs **no purchase and no target
date**, so the pivot can actually start instead of waiting on the five open questions. Nothing is bought, applied for, or scheduled.
Everything here is free; the only paid decision (which route, if any) comes
at the end of the week, once the diagnostic says where she's weak.

## The idea in one line

Diagnose first, then study heaviest-first. The exam is 115 questions
(90 scored, 25 pretest), 135 minutes, pass 500 on a 100–700 scale, HRCI
pass rate 47%. The two heaviest domains are more than half the scored
questions, so week one spends its time there — and the diagnostic at the
start tells her whether she already knows them.

## Week layout — Mon Nov 23 to Sun Nov 29

Sessions are movable; the order matters more than the day. Suggested:

- **Mon 11/23 — Session 0, part 1:** download the HRCI outline + Davis cheat sheet, start the free mock.
- **Tue 11/24 — Session 0, part 2:** finish the mock, score it **by domain**. That split is the whole point of the week.
- **Wed 11/25 — Session 1:** Compliance and Risk Management (29%).
- **Thu 11/26 — Session 2:** Employment Lifecycle and Employee Relations (26%). Note: Thanksgiving Day — move it to the weekend if you'd rather not study that day; the week still works with a skipped day.
- **Fri 11/27 — Session 3:** Compensation / Wage and Hour (21%).
- **Sat 11/28 — Session 4:** Leaves of Absence and Benefits (14%).
- **Sun 11/29 — Session 5 + end-of-week decision:** Health, Safety, and Workers' Comp (10%), then the route decision below.

## Session 0 — Diagnostic (~60–90 min, split across two sittings if needed)

- Download the free HRCI exam content outline (PDF) — the skeleton for
  everything:
  https://www.hrci.org/docs/default-source/web-files/2021-phrca-exam-content-outline.pdf?sfvrsn=37994461_2
- Download Alicia Davis's free cheat sheet, "Pass the PHR-CA: What to Study
  First (and How to Study Smart)":
  https://www.aliciadavishrconsulting.com/s/PASSTHEPHR-CAWHATTOSTUDYFIRSTANDHOWTOSTUDYSMART_20260422_001253_0000-2.pdf
- Take the free PracticeTestGeeks PHRca mock (90 questions, no sign-up):
  https://practicetestgeeks.com/professional-in-human-resources-california-phrca
  Do it as a diagnostic, not a test: mark every question you're unsure of,
  even the ones you get right, and score yourself **by domain**, not just
  overall. That domain split is the whole point of the week.

## Sessions 1–5 — one domain each, ~45–60 min, heaviest first

Work through in this order. Each session: read the outline section for the
domain, work the free questions on it, then write down the three laws or
thresholds you keep missing. Sources are the actual California agencies —
the law as written, not a third party's summary of it.

1. **Compliance and Risk Management — 29% (~26 of 90 scored)**
   California Civil Rights Department (FEHA discrimination, CFRA leave,
   harassment prevention) + DIR (wage/hour enforcement, retaliation).
   This is the domain the 47% pass rate is mostly about.
2. **Employment Lifecycle and Employee Relations — 26% (~23)**
   Hiring, onboarding, discipline, termination, employee relations — same
   CRD/DIR sources, plus the outline's lifecycle checklist.
3. **Compensation / Wage and Hour — 21% (~19)**
   DIR: minimum wage, overtime, meal/rest breaks, final paycheck rules,
   exempt vs non-exempt. The most rule-dense domain — flashcards help here.
4. **Leaves of Absence and Benefits — 14% (~13)**
   EDD: State Disability Insurance, Paid Family Leave, unemployment;
   CRD: CFRA vs FMLA differences (the classic California trap).
5. **Health, Safety, and Workers' Compensation — 10% (~9)**
   DIR / Cal/OSHA: injury and illness prevention, workers' comp basics.
   Smallest domain — don't let it eat a whole evening.

Free practice between sessions: PracticeTestGeeks free tests and flashcards
(same page as the mock), and the Study.com PHR-CA/SPHR-CA guide
(video + text lessons, quizzes, flashcards) via Class Central:
https://www.classcentral.com/course/study-com-phr-ca-sphr-ca-certification-exam-pract-112942

## End of week — the decision this earns

Score the diagnostic by domain and compare with how sessions 1–5 felt:

- **Weak in Compliance and/or Lifecycle (the 55%):** structure is worth
  paying for. Middle route — Alicia Davis 2026 guide, $67.99 (verified
  2026-11-14 on her own site) + $150 HRCI practice — is $713 all-in
  ($495 exam + $67.99 + $150). Full structured route — HRCI Cert Prep
  $449 bundled + $150 practice — is $1,094 all-in. Both need a date first:
  Cert Prep access lasts 180 days from purchase, so don't buy until the
  exam date is set and within ~6 months.
- **Strong across the board, weak spots narrow:** lean route — $645 all-in
  ($495 exam + $150 HRCI timed + untimed practice, the only practice
  written by the exam body). Keep studying free from the outline + DIR /
  CRD / EDD until a date is set.
- **Strong everywhere:** the date is the only remaining decision — pick it,
  then buy the $150 HRCI practice bundle to finish.

Either way, the week also answers the starter pack's open questions from
experience instead of guessing: real weekly study time (count the sessions
she actually did), preferred format (questions vs reading vs video), and
starting point (the diagnostic, by domain).

## What NOT to buy this week

- HRCP Complete Program ($270–$480) — federal PHR/SPHR coverage per
  HRCP's own catalogue; no PHRca edition found. Verified 2026-11-13.
- Generic PHR prep or exam-dump sites — built for the federal exam, or
  unverified questions.
- Anything with a 180-day clock (HRCI Cert Prep, HRCI practice exams)
  until the exam date is set.

## Caveats

- Alicia's 2026-edition guide page says its law updates are for 2025.
  HRCI holds candidates to the laws in effect in January of the exam year,
  so if she sits in 2027, check the 2026 California law changes before
  relying on it. Her coaching is separate: 1.5-hour session $95, two-hour
  masterclass $199 (from her site's coaching PDF, found 2026-11-14).
- The free mock and free guides were verified as free and available on
  2026-11-13; re-check before relying on them if a link fails.
- This plan assumes no prior PHRca study. If Kelly already holds the PHR
  or has California HR experience, sessions 1–2 may go fast — that's the
  diagnostic doing its job, not a flaw in the plan.
