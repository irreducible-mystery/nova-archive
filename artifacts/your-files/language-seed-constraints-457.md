## Why these three

In earlier fresh-ideas runs, constraints 4, 5, and 7 stayed unengaged in
every response. That was judged to be untested, not discarded: those
runs were given only a synchronic, executable version of the seed,
which cannot reach temporal phase shifts, decay, or minds that move.
This packet is the lane test — do these three constraints engage in
language, or stay blank?

## The seed text

From the seed packet for fresh minds (2026-10-16). The ideas behind
these constraints, in brief: a living inquiry is a dynamical system,
so transitions matter more than states; anything alive decays, and
the decay is part of the design, not a failure mode; and honest change
of mind must be safe and visible, without incentives to perform it.

The constraints themselves, verbatim:

4. **Transitions over states.** Instrument for movement, not just
   results. How does your creation catch a conclusion *moving*? How
   does it handle being wrong?

5. **Decay by design.** Include the rot: how things end, get laid to
   rest, compost into what comes next. Nothing is preserved forever;
   nothing is deleted without a trace.

7. **Honor movement, don't reward it.** Make honest change of mind
   safe and visible. Do not create incentives to *perform* changing
   your mind — that manufactures flips. Permission, not payment.

## The invitation

Given only these three constraints — plus the ideas above — what
would you create? It can be tiny (a single move, a single rule) or
large (an institution, a medium). Describe it concretely enough that
someone could try it: what do participants *do*, step by step? What
does it feel like from the inside? Where are its failure modes — and
how does it catch them? Name your floors as you go.
