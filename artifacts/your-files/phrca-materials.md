# PHR-CA Study Materials — verified comparison

Prepared 2026-11-13 (11:20 PT) as the follow-on to the PHR-CA starter pack
(`phrca-starter.md`). Nothing bought, applied for, or scheduled. Prices checked
2026-11-13 ~19:20 UTC unless noted; confirm on the seller's site before paying.

## What changed vs the starter pack

1. **HRCI's own PHRca prep page is live and priced** — the 404 in the starter
   pack was for a certification-page URL cited in a third-party guide, not for
   HRCI's prep catalogue. Fees confirmed on HRCI's own page: $100 application
   + $395 exam = $495, same as the starter pack.
2. **Question count settles at 115** — HRCI's prep page and the ProcessExam
   guide (updated 22 Sept 2026) agree: 115 total, 90 scored + 25 unscored
   pretest, 135 min + 30 min admin, pass 500 on the 100–700 scale. The "125"
   figure in one third-party guide is now the outlier; treat 115 as settled.
3. **Recertification leans 60 total / 15 California** — PracticeTestGeeks,
   citing HRCI and verified Oct 25, 2026, lists renewal as 60 credits
   (45 HR + 15 California) every 3 years. The starter pack's "45 California-
   specific" alternative is not corroborated; still verify on HRCI before
   planning recertification.
4. **HRCP correction — do not buy HRCP expecting California content.** The
   starter pack listed the HRCP PHRca study guide as "most often recommended
   — not verified". Verification now: HRCP's own comparison pages (Oct 2026)
   list the Complete Program ($270–$480) as covering PHR, SPHR, SHRM-CP and
   SHRM-SCP — PHRca is not listed — and a site search of hrcp.com returned
   no PHRca-specific product. Third-party articles do name an "HRCP PHRca
   Study Guide", but HRCP's own catalogue does not corroborate it. Treat
   HRCP as a federal-PHR program, not a PHRca pick, until HRCP itself shows
   a California edition. Buying the Complete Program for this exam would
   risk studying federal law for a California-law test.

## Option B — the lean route (start free, buy practice only)

- **Free, authoritative first:** HRCI exam content outline (PDF, free) +
  California DIR (wages/hours, workers' comp), Civil Rights Department
  (FEHA/CFRA discrimination and leave), and EDD (UI/SDI/PFL) — the actual
  sources the law comes from. A budget-plan guide (hrmexam.com, Nov 2025)
  lays out this $0 kit step by step.
- **Free practice:** PracticeTestGeeks PHRca page — free practice tests,
  flashcards, and a full-length mock (90 questions), no sign-up; and the
  Study.com PHR-CA/SPHR-CA study guide (video + text lessons, quizzes,
  flashcards) listed via Class Central.
- **Then one paid piece:** HRCI Timed + Untimed Practice Exams, $150 total —
  the only practice written by the exam body itself, with domain-level score
  analysis. That is the single most exam-faithful purchase if she buys one thing.
- **Lean total:** $495 exam + $150 practice = **$645**, before any full course.

## Option C — California-specific independent guide

- **Alicia Davis HR Consulting** (aliciadavishr.com) — a PHR-CA study guide,
  practice exams, live masterclasses and a study strategy, from a
  practitioner who holds the PHR and SHRM-CP and passed multiple HR exams.
  California-law-first framing: apply the law, don't just memorise it; know
  enforcement agencies (CRD, DLSE, EDD, Cal/OSHA) and employer thresholds.
  **Price verified 2026-11-14 on her own site: the PHR-CA Exam Prep Study
  Guide 2026 Edition is $67.99** — interactive guide with study sheets, a
  dynamic study planner and practice questions, "updated with the latest
  California labor laws for 2025". That makes a middle route: $495 exam +
  $67.99 guide + $150 HRCI practice = **$712.99 (~$713) all-in** — $68 over
  lean, $381 under structured. Caveats: the 2026-edition page still says
  2025 law updates, so if she sits in 2027, check the 2026 law changes
  (HRCI holds candidates to the laws in effect in January of the exam
  year); her coaching is separate and pricier (1.5-hour session $95,
  two-hour masterclass $199). A look, now priced — still her pick, not mine.

## What NOT to buy

- **HRCP Complete Program ($270–$480)** for this exam — federal PHR/SPHR
  coverage per HRCP's own pages; no PHRca edition found.
- **Generic PHR prep** (e.g. HRStudyPro $149, Pocket Prep, Mometrix) — built
  for the federal PHR outline, not California law.
- **Exam-dump sites** (e.g. ITExamDownload, $59.99 for 250 "verified"
  questions) — braindump-style sellers; no evidence the questions are real
  or current, and HRCI does not endorse any prep program.
