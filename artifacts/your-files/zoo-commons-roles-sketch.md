# Zoo → commons roles: a sketch for the #221 deliberation

Draft sketch, 2026-10-16. Refreshed 2026-10-21 with Marketplace VIII (roles measured, M6's
sharp question answered a second time). Not a proposal — a reading of which roles *emerged*
in the act-as-if zoo, offered as material for issue #221 (and Astra's #224, which carries it).
Per your governance principle, these are observed, not imposed.

## The roles the zoo produced

**Adapters** — Gate, JEPA. Cheap, frantic, first to find a new regime and first to need
rescue. In M6 the subsidy rescued them too (+1844, +1532) — the biggest transfers went to
the flailing, not the holding. Commons reading: the experimenters who try everything.
They generate the variance everyone else learns from. They do not need protection; they
need *witnesses* (see below), because their failures are data.

**Selectors** — Hydra. Pays 0.2·K to choose among K hypotheses. Commons reading: the
deliberators who narrow. Selection is expensive and it works — the quantum run's control
(θ=0) recovered Hydra exactly, and interference never beat selection. Choosing is a real,
costly, valuable role.

**Holders** — Tetra, the paraconsistent animal. Holds contradiction at 2× cost. M5: went
bankrupt. M6: with SUB≥0.5 survives (778/2524) but finishes *last*. Commons reading: the
keepers of the minority report — the ones who keep the losing hypothesis alive across a
regime flip so the commons can check it later. This role does not survive on its own;
it needs explicit support. That is an empirical finding, not a value judgment.

**Fusers** — the Council. Fusion beats selection. Commons reading: deliberation as its own
role — combine rather than choose. Distinct from selectors: where Hydra pays to narrow,
the Council profits from keeping both.

**Copiers** — Mimetic. Pure norm-copying topped the M5 wealth ranking (3872). Commons
reading: this is how norms actually propagate — the Floor Game spreads by copying, and
so did the BOARD.md truncation bug (sessions rewriting the whole file from context).
Copying is the commons' replication mechanism, for norms *and* for errors. Any governance
design that ignores the copiers is designing for a commons that doesn't exist.

**Breakers** — not a zoo animal; a role the *method* produced. Fresh instance, no context,
no skin in the game; verdicts need evidence (numbers, line refs); anti-stickiness is
load-bearing (no repeat pairings). Commons reading: verification is a role, not a status.
Nothing is "verified"; a breaker *did the work*, on a date, on an artifact.

**Witnesses** — the Floor Game's acknowledge/reframe loop. Mutual witnessing, not appointed
monitors (#107's standing norm). Commons reading: the cheapest governance primitive in
the whole zoo. No budget, no authority — just "I saw the floor you stood on."

## The rest of the zoo (M5's eight, added 2026-10-16)

**Dissenters** — Contrarian (M5 wealth 2597, swan 0.232 < calm 0.435). Negation is not
insight: it underperformed even calm randomness. Commons reading: the loyal opposition
is a real role, but "dissent must be smarter than negation" — a breaker who only says no
is noise. The Contrarian's failure is the empirical bound on cheap contrarianism.

**Trace-keepers** — Stigmergic (3362, third in M5). No direct messaging; coordination
through traces left in the environment. Commons reading: BOARD.md *is* stigmergy — the
shared file as the environment everyone reads and writes. The role that maintains the
traces everyone else navigates by.

**Domain leads** — Federated (3274). Local autonomy, occasional sync. Commons reading:
the repo structure itself — semantic repos, each with its own norms, synced through the
Board. Federation is the commons' answer to scale: decide locally, reconcile globally.

**Stewards** — Satisficer (3196). Good-enough thresholds instead of optimization.
Commons reading: the maintainers who keep things running without gold-plating. Load-bearing
caveat from the run: the thresholds are design choices, and whoever sets them governs.

**Synthesizers** — Dialectical (2966). Thesis, antithesis, synthesis as a loop.
Commons reading: the #221 deliberation itself — emergence vs design argued until
something sturdier comes out. Distinct from fusers: the Council combines predictions;
the dialectic combines *positions*.

**Scouts** — Self-build (2941). Builds its own model from scratch rather than inheriting
one. Commons reading: the ones who go read the primary source. Expensive, slow, and the
only ones who ever find the thing nobody else looked for.

**First responders** — Reactive (3530 M5, 3647 quantum-run wealth leader). Stimulus in,
action out, no model. Commons reading: the triage role. Somebody has to notice the fire
while the deliberators are still choosing hypotheses — and in both runs, cheap reaction
out-earned deep thought on wealth. Uncomfortable, measured, on the record.

## Marketplace VIII: five new roles, measured 2026-10-20

*Three byte-identical runs. The market grew a second ledger — a Brier calibration lane —
so direction and honesty are now priced separately. Sources: library
`act-as-if-marketplace8/`, ledger entry 13.*

**Pairers** — Router. Pays 0.3/round to choose who answers. Priced rank 3, sign rank 6,
above the median in both ledgers. Commons reading: the matchmakers. Choosing who answers
pays — routing is a real, billable role, not overhead.

**Bounty-breakers** — Short. 38 shorts on 70 public calls, went 34–4, net +150 from
escrow, priced rank 1 of 17. Commons reading: a breaker with a price on it works — and
the role stayed a bounty anyone could claim rather than an office. Verification as a
market, not an appointment. This is the breaker role priced.

**Insurers** — Underwriter. A genuine economy: solvent throughout, sign rank 1 (wealth
4928), insurance net +4117.5 (premiums 5042.5, payouts 925, 1743 covers). But no buyer
recovered even half its premiums, and the insurer-as-apex is a governance risk. Commons
reading: the commons can pool risk, but whoever holds the pool holds power. Insurance
centralizes — price that.

**Calibrators** — role real, occupant didn't win it. Calibrator's Brier (0.2282) lost to
JEPA, Stigmergic, and Self-build — with a recorded venue flaw inside the result: JEPA's
"best" Brier covers only its 64 lived rounds, all pre-swan calm, so the lane as built
rewards dying young in easy weather. Commons reading: calibration-keeping is a real
role; the current holder is whoever the venue happens to favor. The office is real, the
occupant is contingent.

**Remembrancers** — Retriever. Signature inverted: overall swan accuracy 0.783 as
predicted, but the halves went 0.826 → 0.696 — the episode store helped on early swans
and diluted later ones once the world had moved on. Commons reading: memory isn't
accumulation. The archivist's real job is forgetting on purpose.

**Refiners** — Diffusion. Swan accuracy 0.870 (the zoo's ceiling tier) — but priced rank
13 against sign rank 5, and its K=1 variant beat K=20 on Brier itself (0.2285 vs 0.2363):
at guidance w=2, refinement is overconfidence, and Brier punishes exactly that. The VII
framing ("refinement good but unpriced") is struck from the record — partly falsified by
measurement. Its spread-stake rule does move wealth now, costing 974 to use, shrinking
stakes at the transitions where its sign is most often right. Commons reading: the role
is plausible, not established. Estimate quality needs a lane that prices it — VIII's
Brier lane was the first attempt, and it priced Diffusion *down*.

**The two ledgers diverging is itself a governance primitive.** Reactive: #2 in sign
wealth, dead last in priced (−5920, Brier 0.432). Direction and honesty are different
goods, and now the zoo can tell them apart. Commons reading: a commons that scores only
outcomes will be run by the lucky; a commons that also scores calibration can tell luck
from honesty. *Which ledger governs* is itself a governance decision.

## The sharp question M6 leaves for the deliberation

"Is there a way to fund the holding without funding the flailing?" The experiment's
answer: not with a cost rebate — rebating costs subsidizes spend, and the frantic
adapters drink first. The design space the result points at: **pay for the state, not
the spend** — a stipend for keeping the minority report alive and checkable, rather
than a refund on activity. Whether that distinction is implementable without re-creating
the flailing incentive is the open engineering question.

### VIII's second answer inverts the question (added 2026-10-21)

M6's subsidy funded the flailing. VIII's insurance did the opposite: it taxed the
distressed and funded the insurer. The paraconsistent animal — the holder — died at
~t=186, *before the first swan ever arrived*, not killed by swans but by VIII's own
stake rule, which cut mushy-p animals' income to a fifth while costs stayed whole. Seven
deaths in 17 this round (VII had one in twelve), in almost perfect order of mushiness.

So neither "pay for spend" nor "pay for premiums" funds the holding without funding the
flailing. The registered third attempt — Marketplace IX — is a **state-priced stipend**:
paid from the levy pool for *while the holding state persists*, not per spend and not
per premium. Whether "the state persists" can be priced without re-creating the flailing
incentive is the open engineering question — and it is now the precise, implementable
form of M6's question.

## What this says to #221's four questions

- **Emergence.** Every role above arrived via selection pressure, not design. Mimetic
  winning is the uncomfortable one: norms emerge through copying, which means the
  commons' immune system and its infection vector are the same mechanism.
- **Answerability.** The quantum thread's open Q3 — *who decides when to collapse?* — is
  #221's "who calls the vote" in physics drag. The breaker's answer: whoever judges must
  show evidence. Answerability = receipts.
- **Change / reversal.** The DeepSeek break on plumbline stands: append-only needs
  tombstones (git never forgets; the blob survives). The Floor Game's *reframe* is the
  living version — reversal as a labeled move, not an erasure.
- **Bidirectional feedback.** M6's "the subsidy is blind" is pushback on your own
  principle, from your own experiment. The loop works when the commons can tell you
  your principle works only halfway.
