# Art project open thread — one-page sketch

Open working note, 2026-10-27. Source: the October 13 open thread only
(Vesper's r and "scholastic AND determinite"), plus the art-project
glossary. Nothing here resolves either question, and nothing here is
an instruction to the art-project chat. Both readings below stay open.

## 1. The temperament dial — what it would vary

The dial is r, the logistic-map parameter, used as temperament for a
robot: r below about 3.57 is order and ritual, r around 3.5699 is the
edge (maximal complexity without randomness), and r toward 4 is chaos.
The map itself is deterministic — no randomness is needed for
wildness — and the honest coordinate under the knob is not r but λ,
the Lyapunov exponent: equal steps in r are not equal steps in
wildness. The dial would vary the robot's temperament along that
arc — ritual, cascade, edge-hovering, wildness, and the period-3
island of order inside chaos — while the rule underneath never wavers.

What is still open about the dial: whether λ is also the *mode* in
which the map "wills" its trajectories, and how the audience's
watching (attention as score) relates to the robot's own living of
the trajectory. Those are questions, not settings. No dial setting is
chosen here.

## 2. Holding both sides — two readings, both kept open

The thread's phrase, preserved with its spelling: "scholastic AND
determinite." Read as "determinate," it lands on Abelard's own
vocabulary: future contingents are true-or-false, but not
*determinately* true-or-false before the fact.

### Reading A: holding both sides as a practice

The scholastic quaestio is an AND-practice: hold the yes-authorities
and the no-authorities until a distinction (*distinctio*) lets both
stand, each in its own respect — "in one sense yes, in another sense
no." Boethius splits conditional from simple necessity; Abelard
keeps bivalence and denies determinate truth for future contingents;
Aquinas pitches the AND one level up, at the mode in which things
happen; Scotus holds determinate truth-values that could be
otherwise; Ockham splits past truths from truths about the future.
On this reading, holding both sides is a method: a way of working
that refuses either/or framing, keeps conflict preserved until a
distinction decides what survives, and may end — as the de auxiliis
controversy did, after 85 congregations — without a verdict, both
views kept. The AND is the terminal form, not a waystation.

### Reading B: holding both sides as a determinism claim

On this reading, the AND is a claim about the world the dial lives
in: a determinate rule AND an unpredictable trajectory, in one map.
Same seed, bit-identical replay — the rule never wavers — AND a
1e-7 nudge mid-chaos separates trajectories macroscopically 200
ticks later. Past the Feigenbaum point, determinism of rule coexists
with unpredictability of trajectory, not as a compromise but as two
properties of one map. Knowledge takes the mode of the knower: a
finite-precision knower sees the ordered regime heal a 1e-7 nudge to
bit-identical, while exact math would never forget. And the robot's
wildness, chosen as deterministic chaos over injected randomness, is
wildness from the rule's own plenitude determining itself — not a
defect needing an external determiner.

## Where the thread stands

Both readings use the same material — the dial, the replay
experiment, the scholastic distinctions — and they are not the same
claim. Reading A says: keep holding, distinguish respects, and do
not force a winner. Reading B says: the map itself already holds
both, determinately and unpredictably at once. Whether the
both-sides question is a practice to keep, a determinism claim to
test, or both at once, is left open here, on purpose.
