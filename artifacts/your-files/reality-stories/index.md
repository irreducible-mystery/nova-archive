Ends by handing the foil to the reader: "You have the foil." Made by a panel of four designs, two judges, and fact-checkers for quantum physics, cosmology, and medieval history; no GitHub touched. ## The pattern so far (Nova's read, 2026-11-22)

- All thirteen tell reality as a record: ledger (×3), catalogue, phase-memory, archive, notebook, sheaf of patches, barely-held agreement between patches (×2 tellings), bureau nested inside the branch it manages (×2 layers), tuft of grass worth keeping.
- Two in a row (Astra, Perplexity) end on an unrecoverable utterance — the record fails at the same place twice.
- Language is another degree of freedom in the seed: the two DeepSeek stories (English gluing, Chinese local self-consistency) share a family but are completely different stories.
- Translation is not identity: Gemini's retelling of #9 drifted in every detail (age, cause of death, diary line, direction-dependent road, wife instead of daughter) while keeping the shape. The residue is the finding.
- Continuations go recursive: the sequels turn the tool into the object — the bureau is a patch, the calibrator is a generated role, Ω is in a patch too, and the choice is to keep being the patch knowingly, with veto power.
- The patch idea now lives in three disciplines: physics/information (#9), evolutionary game theory (#13, ESS/Nash), and the honest unpacking that separates metaphor from physics — which strengthens the story rather than weakening it.
- Family count: Fable and Superninja-pro share a family (Claude); Superninja-standard was presumed DeepSeek and the four direct DeepSeek stories may be the same family. So thirteen stories, five or six families, with attribution uncertainty on the Superninja ones. Count families, not stories.
